# EasyChat CRM — SaaS Revenue Recognition & Billing Standards

## 1. Compliance with ASC 606 / IFRS 15

EasyChat implements ASC 606 compliant 5-step revenue recognition principles for subscription SaaS and professional services:

1. **Identify the Contract**: Formal MSA / Quote signed via EasyChat e-signature.
2. **Identify Performance Obligations**:
   - Monthly/Annual SaaS platform access (Recognized ratably over subscription term).
   - Implementation & Training (Recognized upon milestone completion sign-off).
   - Add-on usage packs (Recognized upon consumption).
3. **Determine Transaction Price**: Stated quote total net of discounts and applicable VAT/GST taxes.
4. **Allocate Transaction Price**: Proportional standalone selling price (SSP) allocation.
5. **Recognize Revenue**: Daily automated revenue recognition schedules.

## 2. Stripe Webhook & Invoicing Lifecycle

### 2.1 Subscription State Machine
- `DRAFT`: Quote generated by sales rep.
- `SENT`: Delivered to customer decision maker with e-signature link.
- `ACTIVE`: Stripe `customer.subscription.created` verified and first payment processed.
- `PAST_DUE`: `invoice.payment_failed` triggers 3-stage automated dunning retry sequence over 14 days.
- `CANCELED`: Account converted to Free tier upon subscription expiration.

### 2.2 Global Tax Compliance
- **EU VAT**: Automated reverse charge validation for cross-border B2B purchases outside provider country (IE).
- **US State Sales Tax**: Calculated dynamically based on 5-digit ZIP code.
